⚠ AI-blind cards

Forge's built-in AI is hard-forbidden from playing these (AI:RemoveDeck:All in its card scripts) — the simulation scores your deck without them. Lock them so the evolver can't cut what it can't see, and read winrates as "the rest of the deck". A custom pilot is the long-term fix.
